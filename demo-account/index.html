Guarded demo order

Uses the official Create Order (V2) endpoint (POST /portfolio/events/orders) with signed headers. Demo money only. The repo's CI workflow can also execute plans server-side with hard guardrails (max 3 orders, $3/run, buy-YES only, dry-run default) — see scripts/demo_account/README.md.

Strategies (fixed, documented rules — no fitting, no manual input)

  • momentum-jump — buy the jump direction after a ≥ $0.10 last-price move between samples.
  • fade-jump — buy the opposite side after the same trigger (mean reversion). When both fire on one jump the lab takes momentum only.
  • value-ask — buy YES when the ask is ≤ $0.25 and 24h volume ≥ 100 contracts.

Position sizing: 1 contract per trade, max 8 open, max $3 per trade, taker fees from the official schedule (taker = round-up of M × 0.07 × C × P × (1 − P); series multiplier table transcribed from the official PDF; tested against all 42 fee-table cells). Trades are categorised by topic × strategy × entry trigger above.

The high-ROI chat score (0–4): +1 jump ≥ $0.10 · +1 24h volume ≥ 50 · +1 spread ≤ $0.03 · +1 price in $0.05–$0.85. Score ≥ 3 is shown as a pick. This is a transparent heuristic, not a claim of profitability — sample sizes are small and unverified (F-DA-1).
