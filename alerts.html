Live alerting

Two scrolling feeds. All events logs every tracked-market swing of ≥ $0.10 in either direction. High-ROI is the selective feed: those moves (and newly-qualifying markets) that the heuristic potential-return screen rates as high ROI. Causes are not attributed — each entry is marked cause unverified and links to the official Kalshi market.

How the screen works

Potential ROI for a side = (1 − ask) / ask. A market qualifies as high-ROI when the best potential ROI is ≥ 1.5× and the best YES ask size ≥ 100 contracts. Thresholds are configurable in js/engine.js and are a heuristic, not a guarantee (flagged).
