How to read this dashboard

  • Dollar volume = exchange volume_fp × contract notional ($1). It is the size of the market, not anyone's profit.
  • Price is the YES contract in dollars (0–1). The chart X axis is time (seconds → days) and the Y axis is the contract price.
  • Liquidity is measured from the live order book: dollars resting to buy YES or NO, and contracts available at each price.
  • Sharp moves are flagged only when an observed price change is ≥ $0.10. Causes are never asserted; each move links to the market and its official rules.
  • Simulated trades are paper only — see Portfolio and Strategies.
