Events by dollar amount

Kalshi's basic trading unit is the event, not the individual contract, so this is the page that answers "which live event has the most money on it". Each topic is listed separately and events within a topic stay grouped together.

Dollar volume sums volume_fp × notional for the event's markets. It measures how much has traded, not how much is available; use the liquidity column for that.

What "highest dollar amount" can and cannot mean here

  • Computed: traded dollar volume per event, open-interest dollars, and resting liquidity dollars.
  • Not computed: a live "total money at stake" figure. Kalshi's public API exposes volume and open interest, not a pool of committed capital, so this dashboard never claims one.
