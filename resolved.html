Known irregularities on this page (see Flagged):
  • D1 — FIXED (session 6b112481, 2026-11-21). The collector's settled-market fallback had re-applied a topic's title keywords while skipping that topic's own exclusion rules, and matched them as raw substrings, so KXNATGAS15M-… (natural gas — the gas-prices topic excludes it) and KXROMLNBMGAME-…PLO (a Romanian basketball match matched on "Petrolul") were labelled gas-prices. Both rules are now applied — the fallback honours the topic's exclusions and the keywords match on word boundaries — and the browser classifier mirrors the same semantics. The rows below are re-labelled by the next scheduled collection run; until that lands, a stale row may still show the old label, and any row whose topic reads gas-prices but whose title says natural gas is a leftover of the pre-fix snapshot. Source records: KXNATGAS15M-26NOV201415-15 · KXROMLNBMGAME-26NOV201200PLOPIT-PLO.
