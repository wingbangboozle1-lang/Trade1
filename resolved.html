Resolved events — how each market resolved (YES / NO)

Every row is a market the exchange has settled. The Result column is the official result field verbatim (yes or no) — never inferred from the price. The settlement time and settlement value are the official settlement_ts and settlement_value_dollars fields. Each row links the exact official API response it came from.

How it is determined. Kalshi sets result when a market is determined and pays out at settlement. Settled markets appear with status finalized in the REST API (see the market lifecycle doc). Paper positions settle at $1.00 per winning contract and there is no settlement fee (official fee schedule).

 

Archive: data/resolved.json, refreshed by the scheduled collector (scripts/collect.py) from GET /markets?status=settled&min_settled_ts=…; every request is logged in data/log.md. The live dashboard and the Demo Account lab additionally re-check the markets they are watching on every poll and settle open paper positions when the exchange sets the result.
