3. Design principles we will keep

  • Topics stay separate, events stay together. Five topic pages; within a topic, events are ranked and grouped — never mixed across topics in one table.
  • Facts vs opinion. The all-events chat shows observed moves; the model chat shows heuristic picks, always labeled.
  • Blank beats invented. Any value that cannot be confirmed from an official source stays blank and is listed on Flagged for Review.
  • Every row links its source. See Data and Sources.
  • No credentials, no orders. The page never asks for keys and never transmits an order; paper trading is local to the browser.
