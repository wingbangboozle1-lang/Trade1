Kalshi Demo Monitor

Live markets · alerts · simulated strategies — official demo API only

Executive summary. This site tracks live events on the Kalshi demo exchange (official demo REST API) in five topics — gas prices, elections, culture, crypto, and golf — ranked by estimated dollar amount. It runs a live alerting system for price swings ≥ $0.10 (two scrolling feeds: all events, and a high-ROI filter driven by a documented heuristic model), follows tracked events through resolution with the exchange's official yes/no result, and runs automated simulated trades (four paper strategies, official fee-schedule math) with portfolio analytics by strategy and topic. It is 100% client-side: your browser reads the public demo API directly; no credentials are requested or stored; nothing is sent to the exchange.

How the dashboard works

  • Discovery — your browser pages through the official /events and /markets open-market listings (cursor pagination), then classifies every event into exactly one of the five topics using (1) official series whitelists fetched live from /series?category=Crypto and /series?tags=Golf, (2) exact event category match, (3) documented keyword rules. Results are cached locally for 10 minutes to respect rate limits.
  • Ranking — events are ranked by estimated traded dollars (Σ volume × last price) and estimated open-interest dollars; the API publishes volume in contracts, not dollars, so these columns are labeled estimates everywhere (see flagged items).
  • Alerting — the watcher rotates through the highest-dollar events (top 12 by default) every ~2.5 s, comparing each market's last traded price between consecutive observations and against the exchange-reported previous price. Moves ≥ $0.10 in either direction post to the All events feed with links: official API response, event data, demo site, and a news-context search link (attribution explicitly unverified).
  • Model feed — a transparent heuristic (momentum, volume surge, visible liquidity, spread tightness, tradability) posts markets it "likes" with a fee-adjusted estimated ROI when score ≥ 0.62 and est. ROI ≥ 5%. Definitions on Strategies.
  • Resolution — followed events are re-checked against the official settled-markets endpoint; the result field (yes/no) is copied verbatim into the archive. Results are never guessed.
  • Simulated trading — four paper strategies trade automatically against observed top-of-book with official fee math (taker: fees = round_up(0.07 × C × P × (1−P)); maker fills 0 on quadratic series; settlement pays $1/contract with no settlement fee). Portfolio P&L by strategy and by topic on Strategies.
