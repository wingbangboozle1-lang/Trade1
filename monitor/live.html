Scope & safety

  • Public market-data endpoints only; the app stores and transmits no credentials.
  • Simulated trades never reach the exchange; fills are modeled against observed top-of-book with official fee math.
  • Dollar ranking is an estimate (volume × last price); official per-event dollar volume is not published by the API.
  • News links on alerts are context searches, not verified causes.

Data: official Kalshi demo REST API, public endpoints only — no credentials requested or stored. Paper trades are simulated in your browser and are never sent to the exchange. Educational tool, not financial advice. Flagged for Review
