How tracked events resolved — yes or no — straight from the exchange

Resolution tracking. Every event the monitor follows (watched top-dollar events, portfolio markets, alert sources) is re-checked against the official settled-markets endpoint. The yes/no result is copied verbatim from the exchange's result field; if a market has not resolved, nothing is shown. Paper positions settle automatically at $1/contract (no settlement fee per the official fee schedule).

Resolution archive

Each row links the official /markets/{ticker} response containing the settlement result. Results are also available historically per the official Market Settlement documentation ↗.

Why the list can be short

  • Only followed events are re-checked (top-dollar events, alert sources, and your paper positions) — the demo environment settles thousands of markets we deliberately do not fetch.
  • Long-dated markets (e.g. the 2028 VP nomination tracked at snapshot) resolve months or years out; they stay "live" until then.
  • The archive persists in your browser's localStorage, so it grows across sessions on the same machine and browser.
