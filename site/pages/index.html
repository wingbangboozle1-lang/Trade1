What to do here

  1. Pick a topic above. Each topic page lists its open markets, sorted by estimated 24-hour dollar volume, with price, spread, liquidity and close time.
  2. Click a row to chart its last traded YES price (seconds while the page is open, minutes, hours or days) and to see its live order book (contracts available at each price).
  3. Check Sharp moves for price jumps of $0.10 or more. The site cannot verify what caused a move; each row links to the market's official settlement sources so you can check news yourself.
  4. Read Resolved for settled markets and their official yes/no result.
  5. Review the Strategy lab for simulated, rule-based strategies replayed on official candle history, categorised by topic and price band.

Read before using

  • This site cannot place trades and holds no credentials. Portfolio tracking on your Kalshi demo account requires an API key, which was not provided. See Flagged for review.
  • Live refresh uses the public REST API in your browser (polling, not streaming). Public WebSocket channels require authentication, so tick-by-tick streaming is not available here.
  • Demo-environment data may include test or placeholder entries. Those are flagged, not hidden.
