EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

What the site does

The site polls Kalshi’s recommended demo REST API for open markets, applies a clearly labeled text-based filter for gas prices, elections, culture, crypto, and golf, and displays the API’s reported YES prices, sizes, volume, and status with a source link on every market row. It observes YES-bid changes of at least $0.10 between snapshots, shows them in two scrollable feeds, and uses an experimental spread/liquidity screen to produce paper-strategy candidates. It samples a selected-market chart, loads selectable top-5/10/20/50 bid levels per side from the public orderbook endpoint, and follows locally discovered tickers until a yes/no result is returned with settled status.

REST responses may be cached, so polling is best-effort rather than exchange-push real time. Browser-local alerts, chart points, resolution records, and simulated positions are not an account history and do not transfer across devices. The site deliberately leaves aggregate dollar notional blank, makes no ROI claim, and does not assign news causes to price movements.

METHOD AND SAFETY

How to make this more live and decision-useful

Use a trusted server-side bridge for private features

Keep private signing keys in a server-side secret manager, expose a narrowly scoped backend to the static dashboard, and audit access. Do not paste a private key into browser code. Kalshi’s authenticated request and WebSocket guides describe the signing requirement.

Add push updates only after secure authentication

Once a protected backend exists, use Kalshi’s authenticated WebSocket session for ticker/orderbook updates, reconnect backoff, and explicit stale-feed indicators. Keep polling as a documented fallback.

Backtest before claiming ROI

Store timestamped historical snapshots and settlement outcomes, version each strategy, include fees and realistic fills, and compare results out of sample. The current candidate filter is not a return forecast.

Keep price movement separate from cause

The alert's title-based Google News link is a search only, not an ingested or verified headline. Add a timestamped, independently verified news feed before presenting specific coverage, and never imply that a matching article proves causation.

Confirm aggregate notional semantics

Ask Kalshi or locate a documented field that means total traded dollar value at the event level. Until that is confirmed, do not relabel contract counts or a per-contract field as dollar volume.

Preserve resolution provenance

Retain the specific market API response and settlement timestamp/result for each tracked ticker, and visibly mark missing or conflicting status data rather than guessing.
