What the numbers do and do not include

  • Included: entry and exit at the quoted touch, the official taker fee, and $1 settlement payouts when a market resolves on your side.
  • Not included: queue position, partial fills beyond the quoted size, borrowing costs, taxes. There is no exchange account behind these numbers.
  • Fees use the published formula round_up(M × 0.07 × C × P × (1−P)), verified against all 21 rows of Kalshi's schedule — see Methodology.
