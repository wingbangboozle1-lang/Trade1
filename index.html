Executive summary

Kalshi demo is a separate test environment. This static site reads the recommended demo REST API for open markets; it does not handle authenticated portfolio activity. Market discovery is polled every 30 seconds, and prices/order-book quantities are periodically read from the public market endpoints. REST cache delay and browser/network restrictions may affect freshness. The ranking is by reported market contract volume; an official per-event dollar-amount field is not established here, so dollar notional is intentionally not inferred.

Market coverage is restricted by title/category text matching to gas prices, elections, culture, crypto, and golf. Matching is a heuristic and can omit relevant markets or include false positives. Review contract rules and settlement criteria on Kalshi before drawing conclusions. Use the official Kalshi markets site ↗ to inspect a market.
