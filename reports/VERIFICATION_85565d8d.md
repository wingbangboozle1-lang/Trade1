## 3. Irregularities found in this session

**N1 — `data/snapshot.json` is stale and is not regenerated by CI.** *(new)*
- `data/snapshot.json` was last written `2026-11-20T19:11:17Z` (about 9 hours before this session).
- It is built by `scripts/build_snapshot.mjs`, which is not referenced by any workflow in `.github/workflows/` (verified with `grep -rl build_snapshot .github`: no match).
- The only consumer is `scripts/check_site.mjs` (lines 60–80), so the CI check "every snapshot row has an official source URL" runs against this stale file.
- The live pages do **not** read `snapshot.json`; they read `data/topics/*.json`, `data/resolved.json`, and `data/meta.json`, which CI refreshes via `scripts/collect.py` (verified: `assets/app.js:180`, `data.html`, `resolved.html`, `index.html`).
- Its builder also caps the scan: `coverage.open` = 20 pages × 200 = 4,000 events, `truncated: true`, out of 5,974 unique events. Its own counts record `rows_kept 1118` vs `matched 17753`.
- **Impact:** the user-facing data is not affected, but the CI integrity gate checks an out-of-date, incomplete dataset and reports PASS. Owner decision needed: either wire `build_snapshot.mjs` into CI or make `check_site.mjs` check the live `data/topics/*.json` files instead. Requested in COORDINATION.md.

**N2 — The `build_snapshot.mjs` open-event scan cannot reach fast-cycling series.** *(new, related to N1)*
- The scan is limited to the first 4,000 open events (see N1). `collect.py` covers these series by per-category series discovery (`GET /series?category=`), which `build_snapshot.mjs` does not do. If N1 is fixed by switching CI to `build_snapshot.mjs`, gas, hourly crypto, and daily series would be missed. Recommendation: do not switch without adding the series-discovery step.

**N3 — Retracted.** An early reading suggested the gas topic had zero rows. That was a misreading of an empty `markets` key that `collect.py` does not write. The topic file holds the 11 events and their `_markets`. Not a defect.
