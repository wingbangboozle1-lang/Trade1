EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A transparent, read-only monitor

See verified details and open items →

The dashboard discovers open demo markets with Kalshi’s public Trade API, groups markets by five requested topics, records observed YES-bid changes of at least $0.10, and displays bid depth for a selected market. The $0.10 alerts are generated only from consecutive snapshots seen by this browser. The strategy shortlist is an explicit liquidity/spread screen—not a tested forecast or a claim of high ROI.

LOCAL STRATEGY LAB

Automated paper strategies

Automatic simulation is on by default; it creates browser-local hypothetical entries only. No account or order endpoint is called.

Transparent entry rule

Entry screen: observed YES-bid move ≥$0.10, YES spread ≤$0.05, YES bid/ask sizes ≥5 contracts each, market_type=binary, and settlement_bounds_type=default. One position per market/strategy is opened at the API-reported ask and marked at the API-reported bid; these thresholds are experimental, not ROI-validated.

IMPLEMENTATION BOUNDARY

What this dashboard can and cannot verify

The demo REST endpoints and market-data fields are verified against Kalshi’s official documentation. This static client cannot safely hold an API private key, access a private demo portfolio, submit real demo orders, or guarantee push-level freshness. Dollar-notional semantics, strategy ROI, and external news causality remain flagged rather than guessed.
